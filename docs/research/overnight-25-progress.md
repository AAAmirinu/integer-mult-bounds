## Third checkpoint: narrower search and another complex improvement

the transparent side map and central map need at least n total auxiliary
dimensions, giving W>=4n^3 under the shared rank-one compiler. Thus a
2^-25-capable instance in this architecture must have rational dimension
d<=52. A positive core needs binary rank at least five. Full-subset radial
fitting matrices capable of the target reduce to the linear intersection
case, and retaining the canonical incidence row space cannot thin its side
graph. These are scoped conditions, not all-network impossibility results.

and edge ranks. A positive direct-sum control confirms that duplicating old
labels does not improve their exponent. A genuinely better block geometry
and compressed-side frames remain to be supplied.

The [dyadic central factor](dyadic-central-factor.md) is another actual

removes one central scratch role per invocation, retaining the same common

plus orthogonality over F2. Splitting its weighted features gives a binary

frames and the larger side budget make this a useful alternative to the
