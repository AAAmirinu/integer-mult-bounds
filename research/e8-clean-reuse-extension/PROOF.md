# Construction, dependencies and sufficient argument

The fixed complex witness starts from Chafik Boukhalfa's far-retimed E8 word
and applecrusher's two equal-helper substitutions and two frame refinements.
It additionally replaces helper 556 by -2 times helpers 354 and 461, then
helper 576 by -2 times helpers 402 and 478 after compaction. Exact clean
linear forms agree at every substituted read. The backward upper-frame and
forward nested repairs retain copied-total pins and final address caps.
R is 779. The actual new dirty response and cleanup are regenerated: equality
on clean values alone is not the arbitrary-dirt proof.

The signed prefix restoration is the PR390/395 bracket. For 25 disjoint
targets, subtract lambda times the donor before the prefix, emit the exact
source/write-version/frame difference, and restore lambda times that donor
at the common recovery frame. Lambda is +1 or -1. The full rational main
operator and all retained totals agree with the explicit same-width R779
baseline on all 1,019 independent columns. Source/helper subwords agree
exactly, so the new baseline's explicit dirty response and cleanup remain
valid. The changed compiler permits a main Y read only when its target is Y;
helper-to-X remains forbidden. Both signed orientations and private lifetimes
are freshly admitted, with all actual Clifford phases and physical incidences.

The new bit operation differs from the preceding all-dirt reset: a retired
helper only needs its clean X-content zeroed before a new logical helper is
placed in the same slot. An arbitrary residual helper value is allowed. The
new helper basis determines a newly computed initial D compensation. C is
the reversed helper subword, restoring every physical helper, including its
original independent dirt. Each reset is checked exactly before reuse; the
retired slot has no later original use, the new helper has no earlier use,
and copied totals are protected. The combined word is checked over every
independent binary column. It uses 27 fewer helpers (822 to 795), followed
by 26 fixed legal frame moves. Integer signed-prefix bounds, lifting,
copied-center controls, routes and whole-bank packing are recomputed rather
than inferred from the clean calculation.

The complex assembly has dimension 73, 14,691 live and 15,480 physical
families, deficit 2,496, maximum child rank 48 and halving degree two.
The bit assembly has dimension 56, 10,605 live families, deficit 1,512 and
halving degree two. The actual row coefficients fit the common 20,161
reserve. Both finite invoices and both exact rational moment engines retain
the positive 10^-16 fallback. Adjacent 10^-18 coarse grid points are rejected.
The exact outer assembly uses positive eta=10^-24, beta=10^-18, eight finite
bootstrap continuations and a 10^-27 grid; it checks 47 strict constraints
and the inherited invalid eta, beta and adjacent-point controls.

This is a sufficient finite conditional construction. The inherited all-size
