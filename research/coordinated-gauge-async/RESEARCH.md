   a common target level. Marginal, one-at-a-time search misses this saving.

   stages while each physical owner's actual pending interval is respected.
   OpenAI WordScheduling and earlier controller work inspired this route.
3. Response-matroid cancellation: exact donor relations cancel dirty-response
   pivots. The retained42-pivot kernel is preserved while gauges and inventory
   are reconstructed together. More aggressive local matroid variants were
   checked but did not improve the complete price.
4. Bipartite matching decomposes aggregate inventory nominations into literal
   per-invocation owners. The frozen witness, not a solver status, establishes
   that every helper fits and active data families remain distinct.

These are combinations and applications of established ideas; no claim is made
that matroids, matching, scheduling or shared maxima are newly invented theories.
