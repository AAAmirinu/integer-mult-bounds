## Sufficient width-dominance transfer

Suppose an already verified layout assigns complete helper roles of widths w to idle children of ranks r. Each child owns one physical family and one chronological idle interval. A call uses each physical family at most once. Suppose the new source has the same number of roles and a width-preserving injection except that some width-9 roles become width-8 roles. Keep every role's invocation, family and child. Shrink the selected support by one, reorder the disjoint supports contiguously within that same child, and charge the full complement as its new remainder. Reassign local helper indices within each width class using the new source's ordered residual widths. This produces a legal abstract allocation: no width grows, no idle interval changes, and full-family uniqueness is unchanged.

This assertion alone does not identify physical operators. The emitted full ambient owner charts must be rebuilt from the actual selected source and physical pending axes. Their normalized endpoints, dirty-spectator restoration, chronology, distinction within each call, and all leftover axes are independently checked. These are retained proof obligations of the executable verifier, not an assumed new oracle.

For a child with used widths w_1,...,w_k, the remainder is exactly r-sum(w_i). The full paid profile is 72 copies of the local recursive profile plus every positive remainder. Shrinking a role never creates a free saving: the removed unit returns to the remainder bill.
