# Research decisions and reusable routes

The useful change is optimizing a response circuit together with its physical complement, instead of maximizing the number of canceled responses. A locally cheaper provider can need wider helpers and still win after a real allocation pays the extra axes. Lemma 1 exposes the exact trade; Lemma 4 realizes it. This is the selected contribution beyond the public 112-port combination.

The four proof files are sufficient construction results. Their mathematical ingredients connect to response-rank methods, exact adjoint observability from linear systems/program slicing, direct-sum restriction, and majorization plus colored matching. Relevant OpenAI source explorations and local tensor/dirty-workspace ancestry are retained under `vendor/base120/research-notes` and its source ledger. No claim is made that these four proofs were imported from Lean or independently originated all those classical ideas.

Exploratory outcomes retained in `research-notes/`:

- Transferring the earlier S100 circuit to 112 ports loses to the frozen PR473 combination; even 24 legal owner exchanges do not rescue it.
- Twenty same-call exchanges improve the 99-pivot 112-port provider slightly. Six of seven deletion types admit the bounded physical constructor. The selected combined deletion of components 1 and 32 wins after all widths and remainders are paid. A failed bounded constructor is not an impossibility proof.
- Observable slicing removes 420 scalar instructions. Single-frame and coordinated-frame lowering found no additional paid-profile reduction, so original frames are retained.
- Stable-carrier quotient is proved as a sufficient fallback, but all 180 ordered proportional-row candidates fail its strict lifetime rule (135 premature reads and 45 destructive delivery uses). No quotient gain is claimed. This does not exclude other compensated rewrites.
- A K5 architecture screen with 88 calls, rank 89 and fully paid larger stock loses to the selected K4 construction. This nomination screen is not a rational certificate of a global barrier.
- New PR524's carry-graded convolution addresses exponential axis padding but does not deliver a directly instantiable bound under this interface; its remaining routing/table/Schur costs are not assumed free. PR525's cold 102-kernel checkpoint is weaker than the selected public ancestry. Both were read, neither contributes a fabricated numerical saving.

All experiments are bounded. No exhaustive global optimality, four separate numerical gains, new all-size compiler, unconditional multiplication theorem or new Lean proof is claimed. The final package is frozen through PR525; later proposals belong to the next sweep.
