# Exact structural obstructions for the frozen E8 family

Three narrowly scoped negative results identify which structural hypotheses must
change for the next improvement. None is a new supplier or achieved kappa.

* [Center theorem](CENTER-PROOF.md): the monotone side pattern forces at least
  eight scalar center channels. Every nonzero form in the frozen eight-center
  row-space spans all nine binary address axes, as certified by 511 explicit
  nonzero integer minors. Merely rebasing those centers cannot lower their
  copied Fourier rank mass of 72 per local invocation.
* [Pattern theorem](PATTERN-PROOF.md): every nonzero form in any rank-eight
  completion of the monotone side pattern has binary source span at least
  seven. All 391 clique witnesses and the exceptional-normal classification
  are exact. Even replacing eight rank-nine centers by rank-seven centers
  for free misses 0.002 when all other invoice entries stay fixed. Higher-rank
  localized centers and a joint bridge remain outside the theorem.
* [First-frame theorem](FIRSTFRAME-PROOF.md): the original R779 first frames,
  independent helper residual ranks, copied centers, data endpoints and geometry
  cannot reach kappa 0.002, even with free maximal entrances and for any bank
  count. The relaxed coarse root is strictly below
  0.001616652184482181225181. This is an upper bound for that fixed family,
  not an achieved exponent.

These results permit changed first frames, new central completions, joint
endpoints, nonmonotone words and new geometries. They supply no global
impossibility claim. The constructive E8 witness is maintained separately in
[the extension package](../e8-clean-reuse-extension/README.md).

From a checkout with Python 3.11 or later, standard library only:

```sh
python -B research/e8-structural-frontiers/verify.py /fresh/output
```

The output must be new and outside all source packages. Suppliers are read-only.
All exact records are regenerated before comparison with expected results;
absolute host paths are informational and excluded from the comparison.
Supplier byte hashes, original derivation scripts and the unchanged exact
moment engine are pinned. `vendor/` preserves derivation snapshots; portable
producer changes are limited to paths, input hash guards and output handling.
