# Sufficient retiming and exact invoice

Let W be PR364's frozen final word. Replace only the gate frame of each ADD
listed in `selection.json`, then emit each operand's MOVE immediately before
its next use, with the original initial and final frames.

The ordered non-MOVE instructions are identical, including every integer
coefficient, source/destination register, COPY, and erase. Consequently the
formal signed scalar operator is identical for every initial scalar input,
including arbitrary helper values. This is an exact instruction-sequence
identity and does not rely on binary sampling.

Scalar identity alone is insufficient. The replay independently verifies
nested operand paths, the integer-source-span condition at every ADD, each
frame and annihilator rank, nondegeneracy under the inherited Gram form,
COPY lifetimes and dirty helper restoration. The unchanged initial and final
frames preserve each helper's residual block and the literal stock. The
explicit exact bank tiling is replayed. Independent native legality and all
five-stage formal-column checks are also rerun on the changed literal word.

The change in the local paid histogram is

    H1:-17, H2:+1, H3:-19, H4:+35, H5:-34,
    H6:+17, H7:-17, H8:+17, H9:+17, H10:-17.

The coefficients sum to -17 and their rank-weighted sum is zero. Unchanged
COPY costs and the inherited five-stage idle distribution are included in
the full price. The residual width sum is 131850, normalized stock is 125190,
and normalized deficit is 24480.

The exact bit coarse saving is

    c = 402794119831239 / 500000000000000000.

Both independent rational engines prove the full-fallback moment is below
one at c and above one at c + 10^-18. With the unchanged E8 complex supplier
and the inherited outer assembly, all 47 strict inequalities hold for

    kappa = 160987957846297 / 200000000000000000,

and the adjacent kappa grid point is rejected. This yields the stated
conditional sufficient construction under the inherited interfaces.

The logarithmic search objective nominates candidates only. Neither floating
point annealing scores nor a sum of prior published gains is a certificate.
The 35 retained components have negative rational paid-moment intervals at
the baseline exponent; the final claim additionally uses the actual emitted
word, exact stock and complete repricing.

No optimality claim is made for the frame lattice, the gate order, the
construction family, or integer multiplication in general.
