# Attribution and source lineage

campaign and record update: ai-nanachi, prepared with substantial OpenAI Codex
assistance, October 11, 2026. New contributions are Apache-2.0.

The repository is CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds, maintained by Douglas Colkitt.
The upstream conditional OpenAI #109 framework and retained proof dependencies
are not replaced. Original notices and source-specific licenses remain in the
immutable vendor directories, including CC0 mathematical proof sources.

The final package does not claim that ordinary gate retiming or halving degree
two is new. Its new bit search moves connected scalar groups while retaining
their scalar order. Headline assembly parameters are credited to PR #376.
The full `SOURCE-PINS.json` binds each imported byte to its original repository
path, commit and SHA-256; suppliers do not need network access to replay.
