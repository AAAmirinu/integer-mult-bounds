# Attribution and scope

This contribution was prepared with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance.
New material is contributed under the repository's Apache-2.0 license.

It builds on the reviewed composition of DreamingOfClouds' gen4 bit
circuits (PR276) and Rohan Arun's common-delivery frame retiming (the
reviewed PR279 checkpoint). Rohan's retiming uses eumemic's earlier
common-frame technique. The finite stopped-leaf bootstrap is Rohan Arun's
PR185 mechanism; extending its finite depth does not claim authorship of
that mechanism.

The retained source chain includes Henry Grant and Jacob Sussman's
five-stage construction, Evan McKinney and Rohan's completed banks,
Chafik Boukhalfa's physical helper and checkers, Avi Eisenberg's complex
supplier, eumemic's scalar and source527 framework, RaD's balanced
positional transform, Zhihao Chen's and icekylinx's compiler/assembly work,
and the earlier contributors preserved in the original source notices.
The source-specific notices and licenses in `../five-stage-gen4-banks/`
and the repository's `CONTRIBUTORS.md` remain authoritative and unchanged.

The new contribution is a scoped rational-envelope refinement: retain the
unrounded fallback count already derived in the proof, avoid loose
rounding of the bad-class probability, tighten its invertibility
conditioning, explicitly account for disjoint good/bad recursive
branches, and select finite ordinary/outer parameters with exact
certificates. The full physical construction and inherited all-size
interfaces remain dependencies. This is not a new physical circuit,
a formally verified full multiplier, a practical speedup claim or a
claim of priority over stronger concurrent proposals.
